2. Do / Does

QuestionYesNo
Do you ...?Yes, I do.No, I don't.
Does he ...?Yes, he does.No, he doesn't.
Does she ...?Yes, she does.No, she doesn't.
Do they ...?Yes, they do.No, they don't.
الشرح: نستخدم Do مع I / you / we / they، ونستخدم Does مع he / she. في الإجابة القصيرة نستخدم do أو does أيضًا.
⭐ Does he/she play...?   →   Yes, he/she does.

3. More and Most

AdjectiveCompare 2Top of the group
excitingmore excitingthe most exciting
boringmore boringthe most boring
dangerousmore dangerousthe most dangerous
difficultmore difficultthe most difficult
المقارنة بين شيئين: نستخدم more + الصفة + than.
مثال: Rock climbing is more dangerous than jogging.

الأعلى بين مجموعة: نستخدم the most + الصفة.
مثال: Hang gliding is the most dangerous.
👀 ترى than؟ فكّر في more.
🏆 تتكلم عن الأعلى في المجموعة؟ فكّر في the most.
